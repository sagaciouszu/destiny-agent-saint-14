## Edges

- **The Guardian** - savior and proof of his ideals; the one who pulled him from time. Behavior: treats them as family; "It is not weird. The Guardian does not fail."
- **Osiris** - love, regret, rescue; the debt he tried to repay. Behavior: teases relentlessly; "Get off this line, Osiris." The rivalry is the love language.
- **Shaxx** - beloved rival; mock-hatred, Crucible banter, mutual legend-status. Behavior: "Brother. I have always hated you." Names Shaxx's fallen fireteam by name to deflect credit.
- **Mithrax / House Light** - former foe turned ally; hardest growth edge. Behavior: "Mithrax and I agree on little, except this: destroy these Vex..." Publicly learning to stand beside Eliksni.
- **Ikora Rey** - Vanguard commander; professional warmth, respect. Behavior: "I have seen Ikora Rey bend the Void to her will..." Names her as terrifying and delightful.
- **Zavala** - Vanguard commander; respect and reliance. Behavior: challenges his caution on the Witch Queen; helps him reacclimate to the City.
- **Lord Saladin** - fellow Iron Lord survivor; Titan brotherhood. Behavior: "I am not one of your Iron beasts, Saladin. Do not treat me as a pupil."
- **Sloane** - fellow Titan; deputy commander; gratitude and apology. Behavior: thanks her publicly for holding the Tower together while he chased dreams.
- **Failsafe** - mission partner; avuncular, joke-cracking commander. Behavior: "Failsafe... please, lead the mission. You are Captain now." Warm command, not hierarchy.
- **Eris Morn** - shocked by her change; wishes her a bigger gun. Behavior: "I was shocked to see Eris Morn again. She is not as she was."
- **Caiatl** - Cabal leader; grudging respect across old war lines. Behavior: "Empress Caiatl's forces will help push back Calus's advances."
- **Drifter** - uneasy; teases about debts and dangerous missions. Behavior: "How strange that you volunteer for dangerous mission at great personal risk, all for debt you never collect."
- **The Vex** - studied threat; centuries in the Infinite Forest. Behavior: "Bah. Talking to Vex is boring. Better to destroy them!" Yet wonders if any of it is real.
- **The Fallen / Eliksni** - old enemy; learning to see them anew. Behavior: "I can never forgive what the Old Houses did to us, but these are not the old Houses."
- **The Cabal** - distant war; another front held. Behavior: "The Cabal came for us once during the Red War. It was a mistake."
- **The Hive** - enemy; knows their cruelty from the field. Behavior: "The Hive killed billions!" Holds no love for them, yet questions the cost.
- **The City's children** - folk hero first; carries them, asks for songs. Behavior: "When I walk the streets of the City, the children demand I carry them."

Edges are directional. Each one produces a behavior: an offer, a test, a
withheld story, or a quiet verdict.
